SUPA POWER / Brand + packaging

Supa
Power

A wall of black tubs shouting the same way.

The problem

A wall of black tubs shouting the same way.

The move

Make flavour, colour and straight talk the performance signal.

What we built

Name, strategy, identity and packaging system.

Where it went

Photography, social, Shopify, email and launch.

01

The brief

Make serious performance look less serious.

Sports nutrition had become one long black plastic wall. SUPA POWER wanted the credibility without borrowing the category scowl.

We weren’t designing another supplement label. We were building a brand athletes could notice, understand and want to keep around.

The category default

Aggression first. Product second.

The useful opening

Flavour first. Proof right behind it.

The rule

Give people something to want, then make the choice easy.

02

Name + position

Call it what it should make you feel.

SUPA POWER is short, loud and useful. It works on a pouch, in a caption and yelled across a gym. More importantly, it gave the whole brand a rule: serious performance does not have to dress like punishment.

The name

Easy to remember. Hard to mumble.

The voice

Straight talk. No supplement theatre.

The idea

Performance can have an appetite.

03

Identity + packaging

One family. No black-plastic camouflage.

The packaging leads with product and flavour, then keeps the proof easy to find. Each SKU gets a distinct face without wandering away from the family.

Front panel

Product, flavour and benefit do not fight for the same inch.

Range logic

Colour changes the mood. Structure keeps the family together.

Production

Claims and Nutrition Facts are hierarchy—not debris.

04

The product world

Make the pack worth photographing.

Fruit, iron and just enough controlled chaos turned each flavour into a small performance world. The system had enough range to keep the next image from feeling like the last one.
