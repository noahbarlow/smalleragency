SUPA POWER / branding + packaging case study

performance without the category scowl.

We named it, gave it a voice, built the pack and carried the same idea through every place people met it.

strategy · naming · identity · packaging · commerce
the problem

A wall of black tubs and brands shouting the same way.

the move

Make flavour, colour and straight talk the performance signal.

what we built

Name, strategy, identity, packaging system and brand guide.

where it went

Photography, social, Shopify, email and launch.

01the brief

make serious performance look less serious.

Sports nutrition had become one long black plastic wall. SUPA POWER wanted the credibility without borrowing the category scowl.

We weren't designing another supplement label. We were building a brand athletes could notice, understand and want to keep around — from the first pouch to the fifteenth post.

the category defaultaggression first. product second.

Different logos. The same black tub, hard stare and borrowed intensity.

the useful openingflavour first. proof right behind it.

Give people something to want, then make the choice easy.

02name + position

call it what it should make you feel.

SUPA POWER is short, loud and useful. It works on a pouch, in a caption and yelled across a gym. More importantly, it gave the whole brand a rule: serious performance does not have to dress like punishment.

the namesay it like you mean it.

Easy to remember, hard to mumble and built to hold a growing product line.

the voicestraight talk. no supplement theatre.

Benefits in plain English. Enough swagger to be fun. Enough restraint to stay believable.

the brand ideaperformance can have an appetite.

Fruit, flavour and movement became reasons to want the product — not decoration around it.

03identity + packaging

one family. no black-plastic camouflage.

We built a packaging system that leads with product and flavour, then keeps the proof easy to find. Each SKU gets a distinct face without wandering away from the family.

front panel

know it in a second.

Product, flavour and benefit do not fight for the same inch of pack.

range logic

every flavour gets a world.

Colour changes the mood. Structure keeps the family together.

production

made for the real file.

Claims, required copy and Nutrition Facts panels are part of the hierarchy — not debris squeezed in at the end.

04the product world

make the pack worth photographing.

The product shoot turned each flavour into a small performance world: fruit, iron and just enough controlled chaos to keep the next image from feeling like the last one.

05campaign + content

a system with enough range to stay interesting.

Training culture is funny, specific and allergic to marketing fluff. The content could join the conversation without forgetting to explain what the product actually does.

06commerce + launch

the pack started the story. the store had to finish it.

Shopify kept the same appetite, humour and straight talk, then got out of the way when it was time to compare products and buy.
