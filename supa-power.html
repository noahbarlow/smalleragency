case study

supa power.

strategy · identity · packaging · launch
01the objective

bold, unboring, built for performance.

The supplement aisle is a wall of black tubs and shouting fonts. SUPA POWER had to look nothing like it — without losing the serious athlete.

We built an energetic, flavour-led brand with a direct store and enough content to keep the launch moving.

02what we shipped

one idea, everywhere.

We handled the name, packs, store, content and launch together, so every piece looked and sounded like the same brand.

  • brand & asset library + brand guide — social, paid & online
  • influencer & affiliate playbook
  • sell-sheet & catalog
  • e-commerce (shopify)
  • email flows — subscribers, abandoned cart, reviews
03creative execution

one loud idea, all the way through.

the brand

We named SUPA POWER, found its no-BS voice and built an identity that feels at home in serious training without looking like every black tub beside it.

the product

Flavour-first packs, a practical brand guide and an asset library designed to keep every product recognizably SUPA.

the storefront

A Shopify store, product pages and email flows that make the energy easy to buy — from first visit through abandoned cart and review.

the launch

Photography, social, paid media and a creator playbook for CrossFit, bodybuilding, MMA and Olympic lifting. One system, not a pile of posts.

04the campaign system

smart enough to sell. weird enough to stop.

Education, product proof and gym-culture jokes all share the same voice. The feed can move quickly without losing the brand.

05the product shoot

packaging, meet photography.

Fruit, iron and a little controlled chaos. The shoot gave launch, retail and paid media a recognisable world from day one.
