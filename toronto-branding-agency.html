What does a Toronto branding agency actually do?

We help define what a brand should stand for, how it should look and sound, and how that system should behave across packaging, digital and launch. The exact mix depends on what is broken and what the business needs next.

Do you work with companies outside Toronto?

Yes. We are based at 507 King Street East and work with teams across Canada and beyond. The process is built for direct working sessions, whether they happen around one table or one screen.

How is Smaller different from a large agency?

The people in the first conversation stay on the work. There is less coordination to pay for, fewer handoffs to weaken the idea and more access to the people making the decisions.
