The pack is often the most important ad a consumer brand owns.

It has to carry the idea, explain the product, respect the regulations, survive a planogram and still look good in somebody’s hand. That is not a decoration problem. It is a system problem.

We work from the shelf backwards. What needs to read first? What changes across flavours or formats? What can production repeat without slowly wrecking the brand? Those questions shape the design early, not after the hero mock-up is approved.

Questions worth asking

before the first workshop.

Short answers to the questions that usually arrive before the brief does.

What kinds of packaging do you design?

We focus on consumer packaging, especially food, beverage, supplements and other CPG categories. Projects range from a single launch to portfolio architecture across many products.

Can you work with our existing brand identity?

Yes. Sometimes the identity is strong and the packaging system is the problem. We can work inside an existing brand, sharpen what is needed and avoid redesigning things that already work.

Do you handle production files?

Yes. The work is designed to make production, not just a case-study image. We can support dielines, artwork systems, print coordination and supplier handoff.
