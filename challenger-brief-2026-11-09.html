The Challenger Brief / Issue 02

Brands need to earn their premium. Proof is the work.

Five signals on private label, category switching, performance, packaging and the value of being useful.

Week of 09 November 2026Toronto, Canada14 pages

Read on screenDownload the PDFAll issues

The week’s thesis

A credible alternative is a harder competitor than a louder brand.

Consumers are not rejecting brands. Our read of these signals is that the reasons to choose one are becoming more concrete.

A retailer can make value feel credible. An adjacent category can do the same job with a different benefit. A package can win by making the next use easier.

For challengers, the practical question is whether the product, the claim and the experience give someone a reason to return.

01

Private label is becoming a brand system.

Shelf

The story

Walmart Canada is introducing a more consistent Great Value identity across nearly 20 departments and more than 100 categories. Nearly 150 products are expected to transition in 2027, with the wider rollout taking about two years.

The Smaller read

A recognizable system can accumulate familiarity across the weekly shop. Every encounter makes the next product easier to identify. The threat to a challenger is a lower-priced alternative that increasingly looks like a confident brand.

Smaller implication

Include private label in the competitive audit. Test whether your benefit, product distinction and reason for a premium are immediately visible beside the retailer’s offer. Better graphics alone will not establish a better value proposition.

The Smaller take

Private label does not need to be loved. It needs to make your premium feel unnecessary.

Put your pack beside the retailer’s best alternative. Name the difference worth paying for.

Source: Walmart Canada · 27 October 2026.

02

Your competitor may be on another shelf.

Category

The story

Circana figures reported by Business Bearings, citing MarketWatch, put US cottage-cheese sales up 17.7% to about US$2.32 billion. Cream-cheese sales fell 4.4% over the same period. Kraft Heinz has acknowledged the pressure from protein-forward alternatives.

The Smaller read

People buy a way to make breakfast, fill a snack gap or improve a recipe. Those jobs cross category boundaries. A brand can defend its position within a category while the occasion shifts somewhere else.

Smaller implication

Map alternatives by occasion and desired result. Include the adjacent food, the homemade option and the choice to skip the purchase. Research what people switched to, then ask which part of the job it performs better.

The Smaller take

Market share can improve while the market underneath you shrinks.

Ask recent switchers what replaced your product, and what they gained.

Source: Business Bearings · 6 November 2026.

03

Give the lifestyle a reason to exist.

Performance

The story

adidas reported 14% currency-neutral growth in Performance revenue for Q3, against 3% in Lifestyle. Its direct-to-consumer sales grew 17%. The company also described elevated inventory and discounting in lifestyle footwear.

The Smaller read

These are one company’s results, not proof that fashion is finished. They do suggest a useful strategic advantage: demonstrable performance gives people another reason to choose a product and participate in its world.

Smaller implication

Build the positioning around a real activity, a specific benefit or a community that regularly does something together. Product evidence should support the cultural story. The activity must survive when the campaign stops.

The Smaller take

Performance gives lifestyle branding something to stand on.

Identify the behaviour your brand supports and the proof that it improves it.

Source: adidas · 29 October 2026.

04

The workaround is the design brief.

Packaging

The story

General Mills says 90% of cereal consumers take extra steps to preserve freshness, citing a December 2025 Suzy survey. Cheerios with Ziploc PantrySeal adds a resealable inner bag and pouring spout. The US launch began on 27 October.

The Smaller read

Clips, decanting and awkward storage are observations worth recording. They expose friction after purchase that a shelf audit cannot reveal. Packaging is still performing long after it has won attention in store.

Smaller implication

Watch people open, pour, store and use the product at home. Document repeated adaptations before writing the innovation brief. Then test whether the proposed change removes the problem without adding another one.

The Smaller take

If consumers keep fixing your package, they have already shown you where to start.

Observe the next use occasion. Record every extra tool, container and step.

Source: General Mills · 27 October 2026.

05

Count the parts. Then question them.

Systems

The story

California measures SB 54 source reduction by both weight and number against a 2023 baseline. Collective targets are 10% by January 2027, 20% by January 2030 and 25% by January 2032. Individual producers contribute through the approved PRO plan.

The Smaller read

Making a component lighter does not reduce the number of components. That creates a reason to investigate elimination, reuse and refill alongside material optimization. The relevant unit is the whole packaging system.

Smaller implication

For a California-bound product, inventory every covered plastic component and its purpose. Review whether protection, usability and shelf life can be maintained with fewer pieces. Use the applicable reporting guidance to establish which components count.

The Smaller take

A lighter package can still have too many parts.

List each plastic component, its function and whether it can be removed or reused.

Source: CalRecycle · accessed 9 November 2026.

+

Sports-drink codes are entering alcohol.

Hype watch / October signal

The signal

Constellation acquired SpikedAde on 6 October for US$75 million at close, with up to US$278 million in additional performance-based payments. The brand combines sports-drink-inspired flavours with vodka.

The Smaller read

The acquisition establishes commercial interest in the format. It does not establish enduring demand or a performance benefit. Familiar flavours and recognizable occasions may travel; the health associations should not be mistaken for product evidence.

The Smaller take

Study the occasion before copying the costume.

Source: Constellation Brands · 6 October 2026.
