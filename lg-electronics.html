case study / performance creative

make the upgrade feel easier.

Campaign systems for LG appliances that turned practical purchase barriers into clear reasons to act.

offer strategy · campaign creative · digital rollout
Smaller Agency
the problem

Appliance decisions are high-consideration and full of practical friction.

the move

Make the offer solve a real problem, then make it impossible to miss.

what shipped

Trade Up and LG × TaskRabbit campaign systems.

what held

One clear value exchange across every digital format.

01business problem

the product is only part of the decision.

Large-appliance purchases come with practical questions: what happens to the old machine, who installs the new one and how much work sits between purchase and a finished room?

Performance creative had to move beyond feature lists and turn those points of friction into a simpler, more immediate reason to upgrade.

purchase frictionthe old appliance is still in the way.

Haul-away is operational detail, but it can be the detail that keeps a customer waiting.

creative opportunitymake convenience part of the offer.

Lead with the practical value, then let the product finish the argument.

03performance logic
