The short version.

Smaller Agency uses basic website analytics to understand which pages people visit and how they find us. If you email us, we use the information you send to respond and to discuss the work. We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect.

When you visit the site, Google Analytics may collect technical and usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source and interactions with links. Analytics loads after a first interaction or when the page is idle.

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, which may include your name, email address, company, project details and attachments. If you subscribe to The Challenger Brief, we collect the email address you submit and the page where you signed up.

How we use it.

We use website information to understand site performance, improve content and measure whether people can find what they need. We use inquiry information to reply, assess fit, prepare a scope and manage a working relationship if one begins. We use newsletter signup information to send The Challenger Brief and related Smaller Agency updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Services involved.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics for measurement. Formspree processes website forms, including newsletter signups. Email is handled through our email provider. Those services may process information under their own privacy terms and in locations outside Canada.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser, use browser privacy controls or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also ask us what personal information we hold from an inquiry or newsletter signup and request a correction or deletion, subject to legal and operational requirements.
