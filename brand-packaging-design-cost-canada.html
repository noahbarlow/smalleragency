What does brand and packaging design cost in Canada?

There is no honest single number. There is, however, a much better way to understand the quote.

By Noah Barlow / Updated October 21, 2026 / 8 minute read

Smaller / Field note
Scope first.Then price.

Cost guide / Canada / Brand + packaging

Written from the work, not from a content calendar.

A one-SKU refresh and a new bilingual range are not the same design job. The useful question is not “what does a logo cost?” It is “what has to be solved, built and made ready to ship?”

Start with the shape of the job.

Most brand and packaging projects fall into one of three shapes.

A focused refresh

The product and positioning are sound. The pack needs a clearer hierarchy, stronger shelf presence or a cleaner system without throwing away what people already recognize.

A range system

The brand has several SKUs, formats or channels. The work includes architecture, navigation, bilingual information, claims and a repeatable production system.

A launch from zero

The name, positioning, identity, packaging and launch experience need to be built together. This is usually the broadest job because every decision depends on the one before it.

The package is only the visible part.

A quote should make clear whether it includes strategy, naming, identity, front-of-pack hierarchy, range architecture, production files and launch support. If those items are vague, the price is vague too.

The short answer

Cost rises with decisions, formats and handoffs. It does not rise because somebody made the logo larger.

The number of concepts also matters less than people think. Three unfocused routes can create more work than one sharp strategic direction. The expensive part is often indecision, not design.

Seven things that move the quote.

Strategy

Is the positioning already useful, or does the project need research, category mapping, naming and a sharper point of view?

Range size

One pouch is different from twelve flavours across cans, cartons and multipacks.

Regulation

Food, beverage, alcohol, cannabis and supplemented products bring different mandatory information and review needs.

Bilingual copy

English and French need to be planned into the hierarchy, not squeezed into the last available corner.

Production

Real dielines, printer specifications, colour separations, proofs and prepress support are work. They should be named.

Launch assets

Photography, e-commerce, sales materials and retailer decks may sit outside the package, but they determine how the system enters the world.

Decision-making

A small, accountable client team moves faster than a committee collecting preferences.

How to compare proposals without losing the plot.

Ask every studio to show the same things: phases, deliverables, number of SKUs and formats, revision assumptions, production responsibility, timing and what the client must supply.

Then look at the work. Has the studio taken a system from strategy to shelf? Can it explain why the hierarchy works? Does the design survive across a range? A low quote becomes expensive quickly if another team has to rebuild the files before launch.

Questions that come up.

Why do packaging design quotes vary so much?

Because the visible package may be only one part of the job. Strategy, naming, SKU architecture, bilingual copy, regulatory panels, production artwork, photography and rollout can change the scope substantially.

Is a redesign cheaper than creating a new brand?

Sometimes. A focused refresh can preserve useful equity and reduce the work. If the positioning, architecture or name also needs to change, the project is closer to a rebuild.

What should be ready before requesting a quote?

A product list, number of SKUs and formats, target launch date, current dielines if they exist, decision-makers, known regulatory needs and a plain description of what is not working.

Can identity and packaging be developed together?

Yes. For consumer brands, developing them together usually prevents the identity from becoming a system that looks good in a presentation but struggles on a real pack.
