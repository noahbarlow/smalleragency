The studio / Toronto

small enough to notice the small stuff.

Smaller is a brand and packaging studio built around one useful fact: the people with the opinions are also the people opening the files. Noah is in the first meeting, the messy middle and the final press check. No relay team. No “let me circle back with creative.”

AHow we operate

the handoff is where good work goes to die.

So we don't build one into the process.

01

same people. all the way through.

no relay race

You won't explain the brief six times. The people in the first conversation stay close enough to protect the idea — and push back when it needs pushing.

02

the deck has to survive the shelf.

strategy that makes something

If the positioning falls apart on a label, it wasn't very good positioning. We think and make at the same table, with the actual object in view.

03

the team changes. the standard doesn't.

the right gang for the job

An illustrator for one brief. A food stylist for another. Someone who knows an unreasonable amount about foil. We bring in who the work needs — not who happens to be free.

BThe founder
noah barlow · toronto

noah has been doing this long enough to skip the theatre.

Twenty years, eighty-plus launches and more beer, chocolate, cannabis, supplements and frozen dinner than most résumés can comfortably hold. Before Smaller, Noah worked on Miller Lite Canada, Kit Kat Chocolatory, Pilsner Urquell and Carlsberg.

His Brand → Package → Demand framework keeps one useful question in the room: what will make someone notice, believe and choose?
