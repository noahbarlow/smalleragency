01 / How we operate

The handoff is where good work goes to die.

So we don’t build one into the process.

01

No relay race.

You won’t explain the brief six times. The people in the first conversation stay close enough to protect the idea—and push back when it needs pushing.

02

Strategy has to make something.

If the positioning falls apart on a label, it wasn’t very good positioning. We think and make at the same table, with the actual object in view.

03

The right gang for the job.

An illustrator for one brief. A food stylist for another. Someone who knows an unreasonable amount about foil. The team changes. The standard doesn’t.

02 / The founder

Noah has been doing this long enough to skip the theatre.

Twenty years. Eighty-plus launches. More beer, chocolate, cannabis, supplements and frozen dinner than most résumés should hold.

Before Smaller, Noah worked on Miller Lite Canada, Kit Kat Chocolatory, Pilsner Urquell and Carlsberg. His Brand → Package → Demand framework keeps one useful question in the room: what will make someone notice, believe and choose?
