The studio / Toronto

small enough to stay useful.

Smaller is a senior-led brand and packaging studio. The people in the first meeting are the people doing the thinking, designing and making.

AHow we operate

no relay race.

same humans, start to finish

You won't explain the brief six times. The people in the first conversation stay close enough to protect the idea — and challenge it when it needs challenging.

strategy has to make something.

decks are not the finish line

If the positioning falls apart on a label, it wasn't very good positioning. We think and make at the same table.

we build the right gang.

specialists, not seat-fillers

An illustrator for one job. A food stylist for another. Someone who knows an unreasonable amount about foil. We bring in who the work needs.

BThe founder
noah barlow · toronto

noah has been doing this for a while.

Twenty years, eighty-plus launches and more beer, chocolate, cannabis, supplements and frozen dinner than most résumés can comfortably hold. Before Smaller, Noah worked on Miller Lite Canada, Kit Kat Chocolatory, Pilsner Urquell and Carlsberg.

His Brand → Package → Demand framework keeps one useful question in the room: what will make someone notice, believe and choose?
