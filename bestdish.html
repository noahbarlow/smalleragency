case study

bestdish.

strategy · identity · packaging · launch system
01the challenge

building a food category from the ground up.

When Michael Summerfield approached Smaller, BestDish wasn't a company. It was an idea. The challenge wasn't simply creating a brand — it was designing an entirely new consumer behaviour: convincing someone to buy restaurant-quality meals from a freezer inside their own condominium.

We needed a new brand, a new retail experience, a new customer journey, a new trust model, and a completely new go-to-market strategy.

✕

not a delivery app

The market already understood delivery. BestDish has no driver, no wait and no markup added for the ride.

✕

not frozen grocery

These are named restaurant dishes, made locally and tied to real chefs. The freezer had to signal that immediately.

✕

not a vending machine

The hardware looks familiar. The offer isn't: dinner from a restaurant, available downstairs.

every decision had to answer one question: how do you make something unfamiliar feel immediately obvious?

02our role

the brand, the freezer and the first meal.

There was no handoff between strategy and execution. We worked on the idea from the name on the door to the moment a resident opened the freezer.

brand

Positioning, story, identity, voice and guidelines — all centred on iconic restaurant dishes, not frozen food.

experience

Packaging, freezer graphics, website UX, restaurant pages, chef stories and the QR journey. Every touchpoint had to make the new behaviour feel obvious.

launch

First meal free, resident onboarding, building signage, sampling, social content and a repeatable building-by-building rollout.

✳the identity system
03the packaging system

restaurant quality, argued from a freezer.

The pack had to feel restaurant-worthy, survive a real production line and do the selling through frosted glass.

  • meal sleeves
  • labels & nutritional layouts
  • information hierarchy
  • qr experience
  • shelf visibility
  • production system
04go-to-market

building by building.

BestDish doesn't need all of Toronto at once. It needs one building where the freezer becomes a habit, then the next. We built the messages, launch order and resident materials to repeat address by address.

The first meal gets trial. Repeat purchase tells us whether the behaviour stuck.

3pilot buildings, toronto
8–10building proof footprint
1meal free — the first
05the result

not a frozen meal. a new retail channel.

The system is built for a building-by-building Toronto launch: named restaurant partners, premium packs, open pricing and a resident journey that can be repeated at the next address.

06restaurant stories

the chefs are the proof.

Named dishes, named restaurants and real people behind the food. The content makes trust visible before a resident ever opens the freezer.

Restaurant partners at launch: Piano Piano, Pukka, Le Gourmand, Death in Venice Gelato Co. — named dishes, named chefs, every pack.
