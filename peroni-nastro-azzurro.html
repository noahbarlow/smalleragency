case study / national adaptation

italian style, made to travel.

A national out-of-home adaptation that kept Peroni's elegance intact at the scale and speed of Canadian city media.

campaign adaptation · production · out-of-home
Smaller Agency
the problem

Translate a global premium campaign across Canadian formats.

the move

Use restraint as the system: product, light, space, signature.

what shipped

National adaptation, production files and out-of-home rollout.

what held

A consistent premium read from master art to city screen.

01business problem

premium gets noisy very quickly.

Peroni Nastro Azzurro already carried a precise set of equities: Italian confidence, crisp white space, navy, red and the blue ribbon. National adaptation meant respecting those assets while solving for Canadian media plans and production realities.

The work needed to feel considered at every size, including tall digital formats where a conventional crop would have weakened both the product and the atmosphere.

riskmore information. less elegance.

Local requirements can accumulate until the original idea disappears.

responsemake restraint do the work.

Control the reading order and give the brand's most distinctive elements room.

02strategic move
localize the execution, not the aspiration.

The adaptation system preserved the polished global feeling while accounting for new dimensions, sightlines, production specifications and Canadian market context.

03rollout logic
01 / productKeep the bottle heroic.

Product recognition remains clear across every crop and distance.

02 / atmosphereProtect the lightness.

White space and visual calm carry the premium positioning.

03 / signatureLand the blue ribbon.

Peroni's most memorable equity closes every execution.

same italian point of view. new canadian canvas.
