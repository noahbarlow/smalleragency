Taste is invisible. The brand has to make people imagine it.

That means appetite, trust and distinctiveness have to work together. A beautiful pack that hides the product is not useful. Neither is a wall of claims with no character. The best systems make the promise clear and leave enough room for desire.

We think about the whole buying context: the freezer, taproom, shelf, delivery screen, restaurant story and the person scanning a label while holding three other things. Then we design for that reality.

Questions worth asking

before the first workshop.

Short answers to the questions that usually arrive before the brief does.

Do you specialize in food and beverage packaging?

It is one of our deepest areas. Our experience spans beer, frozen meals, chocolate, supplements, maple syrup and other consumer categories where appetite and shelf behaviour matter.

Can you help with naming and positioning before design?

Yes. We often start there. A stronger name or position can do more for the package than another round of visual polish.

Do you create photography and launch content?

Yes. We build the core brand system first, then can direct photography, social, digital, retail and launch assets so the brand enters the market as one coherent idea.
