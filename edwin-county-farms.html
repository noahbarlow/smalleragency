case study

edwin county farms.

identity · packaging system · guidelines
01the brief

a real farm deserves better than clip-art carrots.

Edwin County Farms is a biodynamic farm in Prince Edward County producing organic beef, grain and maple syrup for local chefs and restaurants — and feeding the community through farmers' markets and the on-site farm stand.

They came to us to build the brand properly: a defined style that's honest to their roots (pun intended), clean and simple enough to work everywhere from a syrup label to the website.

02what we shipped

from the fork up.

An identity built around a vintage garden-fork mark and etched-illustration vegetables, carried through packaging and a full set of brand guidelines.

  • brand identity — logo & secondary marks
  • illustration system — vintage etched produce
  • packaging — farm stand labels & goods
  • typography & colour system
  • brand guidelines
03the system

old world on the outside, rigorous underneath.

the mark

A bold serif wordmark split by a vintage garden fork — a modern logo with a nod to old-world farming, built to work on a syrup label, a crate stamp or a road sign.

the illustrations

Etched heirloom vegetables in a single accent red — the texture that keeps kraft paper from feeling generic.

type & colour

Mikela for the headlines, Objectivity for the working copy, and a palette pulled straight from the field: straw, market red, crop greens.

the guidelines

A complete book — vision, logo usage, type, colour and photography — so the brand stays consistent whoever's holding the label gun.
