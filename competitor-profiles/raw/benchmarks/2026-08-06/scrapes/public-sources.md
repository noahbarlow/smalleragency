  - Limitation: published in 2022, not a 2026 survey, and not Toronto-specific.

## Normalized model bounds

- Space demand: 90–115 square feet per employee.
- Gross occupancy: $58–$82 per square foot per year.
- Loaded people cost: $112k–$150k per employee per year.
- Delivery share: 66%–79% of total staff.
- Collected hours: 1,325–1,500 per delivery employee per year.
- Other operating cost: 12%–21% of people cost.
- Profit, media, production pass-through and tax: excluded.

## Rate interpretation

- $200 per hour is retained as a transparent blended planning rate, not a claimed Toronto average.
- Public cards show a repeatable seniority premium: strategy and creative direction can reach $250 or more while blended or production-heavy account rates remain lower.
- The evidence does not support assigning a current rate to any of the ten Toronto agencies except the dated $179 Cossette procurement record. A role-weighted scenario is more defensible than claiming that every agency bills every hour at one rate.
