CPG branding has to work at three feet and three centimetres.

A pack needs stopping power from across the aisle, a clear story in somebody’s hand and enough recognition to survive as a tiny ecommerce thumbnail. It also needs a system that still works after line extensions, retailer requests and regulatory copy arrive.

That is why we do not separate brand strategy from packaging reality. The product, promise, architecture and pack are one connected problem. Treating them that way produces stronger work and fewer expensive corrections later.

Questions worth asking

before the first workshop.

Short answers to the questions that usually arrive before the brief does.

What does CPG branding include?

Positioning, naming, identity, packaging architecture, design systems and the launch tools needed to put a consumer product into the market. The mix changes based on the stage of the brand.

Do you work with early-stage CPG founders?

Yes, when there is a real product, a credible path to market and enough ambition to build the foundation properly. We also work with established teams redesigning or extending a portfolio.

Can you help with retailer presentations and sell sheets?

Yes. Those tools often matter to launch as much as consumer-facing creative. We can build the brand and then create the practical materials needed to sell it into the channel.
