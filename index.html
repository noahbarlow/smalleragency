01what we solve

brands stall when strategy, package, and demand drift apart.

Most brands don't fail at execution. They fail in the gaps: the deck says one thing, the pack says another, and by the time it hits retail nobody remembers the point. We work end to end so nothing gets lost on the way to shelf.

There are no hand-offs here, because there are no departments. You work directly with senior people who've done this before.

02the framework

brand → package → demand.

The order matters. Brand sets the bet. Package carries it. Demand pulls it through.

01brand

Positioning your team can actually use, and that holds up when the category shifts. Get this wrong and everything downstream gets louder, not clearer.

02package

Your pack gets more impressions than any ad you'll run. We design it to win the three seconds you get at shelf.

03demand

Distribution, launch sequencing, retail readiness, and content that turns first-time buyers into regulars.

"packaging is the most under-leveraged growth lever a founder owns. it's media you've already paid for."

— noah barlow, founder, on shelf economics
