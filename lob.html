case study · smlr 012 · bocce mint
lob.
brand identity · illustration · website · venue graphics
01the brief

an old man's game, made impossible to resist.

Bocce has been played in parks for generations — usually by guys in flat caps who take it very seriously. Lob set out to bottle that charm into a social club: courts, food, drinks, and a game anyone can pick up holding a glass of wine in the other hand.

The brand had to do the same trick as the venue — respect the game's history while making it feel like the best night out in the city.

"you can learn more about a person through an hour of play than through a lifetime of conversation."

— plato, patron saint of the brand

02what we shipped

a whole illustrated world.

One mark, one mint-and-red palette, and a custom illustration system big enough to run the website, the menus and the court floor.

  • brand identity — the lob. mark
  • custom illustration system & animations
  • website design
  • venue & court graphics
  • brand photography — vintage bocce archive
03the system

play, drawn by hand.

the mark

A hand-lettered lob. in a solid red circle — part club crest, part court marker, sized for a business card or painted on the floor.

the illustrations

A full cast of players, spectators and geodesic bocce balls drawn in mint, ink and red — with animated toss sequences for the site.

the website

Every page gets its own illustrated scene — home, about, food, even the privacy policy. No stock photography anywhere.

the venue

Court diagrams, floor logos and wayfinding that make the space itself part of the brand.
